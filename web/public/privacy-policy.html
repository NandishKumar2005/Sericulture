Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google, we collect your name and email address from your Google account. We also collect farm-related data you enter such as location, silkworm batch details, and leaf quality scan results.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account securely using Google OAuth 2.0
  • To provide personalised sericulture predictions and recommendations
  • To store your farm data and harvest history

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Your data is stored securely in our database and used solely to provide the ReshmeAI service.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on MongoDB Atlas with industry-standard encryption. Authentication tokens are stored locally on your device and expire after 30 days.

5. Google API Usage

ReshmeAI uses Google Sign-In only for user authentication. We do not access your Google Drive, Gmail, Contacts, or any other Google services beyond your basic profile (name and email).

6. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us. Uninstalling the app removes locally stored data from your device.
